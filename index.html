About T-AG Freight Line

Delivering Logistics Excellence Across America

T-AG Freight Line LLC is a premium nationwide logistics provider founded on the values of safety, transparency, and operational efficiency. Led by Owner **Kemal Gadwah** and Manager **Tara Isardat**, we bring years of industry expertise and dedicated coordination to every single freight lane we manage.

Common Inquiries

Logistics & Compliance FAQ

Find answers regarding driver onboarding, lane coverage, and shipping authorities.

What geographic areas does T-AG Freight Line serve?

We provide nationwide cargo logistics, hauling freight across all 48 continental states. Our dispatch center handles lanes dense in the Northeast, Midwest, and Southeast corridors.

What types of vehicles and trailers make up your fleet?

Our company operates straight box trucks (typically 26ft with hydraulic liftgates) and standard 53ft dry van trailers. We are fully equipped for FTL, regional freight, and LTL dock-to-dock operations.

How quickly do you return lane quote estimates?

Shippers submitting quote requests via our web portal generally receive a calculated rate sheet directly in their email within 30 to 45 minutes during standard dispatch operating hours.

Are you fully authorized and registered with the FMCSA?

Yes. T-AG Freight Line LLC is a fully insured, bonded, and registered carrier. We hold active USDOT authority #4506103 and Interstate MC authority #1783292.

How can an owner-operator apply to lease onto your MC?

Independent owner-operators with straight trucks or semi-tractors can apply using our online Lease-On Application. You will need to upload your CDL license and Insurance COI. Our general manager Tara Isardat will conduct the safety audit and reach out to complete onboarding.
